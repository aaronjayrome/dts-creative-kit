QUESTIONS YOU'LL GET

Answers, so you don't have to improvise

Does this cost anything?

No. No ticket, no card, no donation. You put in a ZIP code.

Is this a Kickstarter?

No. Both films are finished. This is only about getting them onto screens.

What happens after I put my ZIP in?

It matches you to the real independent cinema nearest you and writes an email to their programmer, from your own inbox, that you can read and edit before it sends.

Will it definitely play near me?

No promises. Enough requests from one city and a real programmer takes a look — that's what we can honestly say. Some cities are working, some aren't, and the counts are public either way.

What if there's no indie theater near me?

You get banked for your area rather than pointed at a venue that can't take part.

Why not just put it on streaming?

It'll get there. Theatrical first is the point of the experiment — the argument is that an audience can open a screen without a studio deciding first.

Four things not to say

  • ✕"The studios passed on these." They never went to studios. That's the entire point — you don't need one. Say no studio required.
  • ✕"Get 30 people and the theater books it." We don't promise the booking. Enough people ask and a real programmer looks — and a city can open other ways too.
  • ✕"Tommy Wiseau and Vernon Davis are in both films." They are only in Silt. The six who appear in both: Malcolm Goodwin, Krishna Smitha, Olivia Grace Applegate, David Anders, Michael Papajohn, Isaiah LaBorde.
  • ✕"Horror double feature." Dead Dawn is dark comedy and small-town true crime; Silt is a cosmic thriller. Calling them horror brings horror fans expecting something else.
